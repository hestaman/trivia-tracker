Start a game

Tap Start New Game. Type the video's name and how many questions it has. Pick Just me to play alone, or Play together to play with friends.

Keep score

Answer each question in your head (or out loud). Then tap Correct or Incorrect. It's on the honor system. Tapped the wrong one in a solo game? Use Undo.

Stop early

Tap Stop game at any time. You'll be asked if you want to save your score. If you save it, your history shows how many questions you answered.

Take a break

Tap ‹ Home to step away. Your game waits for you on the home screen. Tap Resume to pick up where you left off.

Play with friends

  • Choose Play together and enter your name.
  • Send the invitation link by text message.
  • Your friend opens the link, types their name and taps Join game.
  • Everyone taps their own answers. The scoreboard updates every few seconds.

Recent players

No sign-up needed. People you play shared games with are remembered by first name for 90 days. In a shared game, tap a name under Invite a recent player and pick them in Messages. Their phone number comes from your phone's contacts, not this app.

See past scores

Tap Game History. For a game you played with friends, tap See everyone's scores.

Where your scores are kept

Solo games are saved on this phone only. Shared games are saved online. Clearing your browser's data erases your solo history.

Put it on your home screen

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
